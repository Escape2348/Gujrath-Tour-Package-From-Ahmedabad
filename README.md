# Gujarat Tour Package: An 8-Day Journey from Ahmedabad

**Meta description:** Explore Gujarat on an 8-day tour from Ahmedabad, with stops in Dwarka, Somnath, Gir, Junagadh, Vadodara, and Kevadia.

Gujarat brings together sacred temples, historic landmarks, distinctive regional food, and remarkable wildlife. An **8-day Gujarat tour package from Ahmedabad** is a convenient way to experience several of the state’s best-known destinations in one trip.

This itinerary combines pilgrimage sites in Dwarka and Somnath with a visit to Gir, time in Junagadh and Vadodara, and a stop at the Statue of Unity. Here’s what to expect along the way.

## What makes Gujarat a memorable place to visit?

Gujarat offers a varied travel experience. In Dwarka and Somnath, visitors can explore important religious sites. Gir is known for its Asiatic lions, while Ahmedabad and Junagadh offer opportunities to discover the state’s history and culture. The journey also includes the Statue of Unity at Kevadia, one of India’s most recognisable modern landmarks.

The variety makes Gujarat a rewarding destination for families, history enthusiasts, wildlife lovers, and travellers interested in spiritual heritage.

## Gujarat tour itinerary: 8 days, 7 nights

The Escape2Explore itinerary begins with arrival in Rajkot and ends with sightseeing and departure from Ahmedabad. The route includes:

### Day 1: Rajkot to Dwarka

Arrive in Rajkot and travel to Dwarka. Visit Dwarkadhish Temple and Nageshwar Jyotirlinga Temple before checking in for the night.

### Day 2: Explore Dwarka and Bet Dwarka

After breakfast, travel to Bet Dwarka and visit the temples and landmarks listed in the itinerary, including Rukshamanee Mandir, Gomti Ghat, Sudama Setu, and Gopi Talab. Return to Dwarka for the night.

### Day 3: Dwarka to Somnath

Drive toward Somnath, stopping at Kirti Mandir in Porbandar along the way. In Somnath, visit the Somnath Temple, known for its coastal setting and religious significance.

### Day 4: Gir National Park

Set out early for Gir, home to the Asiatic lion. The itinerary includes a jeep safari, subject to availability and applicable permits. Overnight stay is planned in Sasan Gir.

### Day 5: Junagadh and Gondal

Visit Girnar Temple in Junagadh, then continue to Virpur and Gondal. The listed stops include Jalaram Temple, Swaminarayan Temple, Naulakha Palace, and the Vintage Car Museum.

### Day 6: Travel to Vadodara

Travel from Junagadh to Vadodara. The drive is listed as approximately seven and a half hours, with the evening free after hotel check-in.

### Day 7: Kevadia and Ahmedabad

Drive to Kevadia to visit the Sardar Sarovar Dam and the Statue of Unity. Continue to Ahmedabad for an overnight stay.

### Day 8: Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar

Explore the listed attractions, including Sabarmati Ashram, Adalaj Stepwell, and Akshardham Temple in Gandhinagar, before departure from Ahmedabad.

*The itinerary and travel times can change. Confirm the final schedule with the operator before booking.*

## Best time to visit Gujarat

The package FAQ recommends visiting between October and March, when conditions are generally suitable for sightseeing. The Rann Utsav is also mentioned on the page as a seasonal event, but it is not part of the itinerary listed for this package. Check dates and route details with the operator if attending the festival is important to your plans.

## What to pack for your Gujarat trip

Bring comfortable walking shoes, sunscreen, a cap, personal medication, and a reusable water bottle. A light jacket may be useful in the evening. Pack a small day bag for sightseeing and keep any valuables secure while travelling.
